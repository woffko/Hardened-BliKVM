Third-party notices for tools/upscale

The lab tools in this directory port two upstream shaders (candidates.js) and load a third at run time. Each comes
from a pinned upstream commit, checked by SHA-256 when it was downloaded:

- Qualcomm Snapdragon Game Super Resolution 1 (sgsr/v1/include/glsl/sgsr1_shader_mobile.frag),
  https://github.com/SnapdragonStudios/snapdragon-gsr commit d926f074bcb9d714e179f1ce0fcb9ee2eeb5074e,
  SHA-256 93e8db987dc79f62e68805f968d63989e5d50daebe4d040e4c7104047ad49139. Ported to WebGL2 in candidates.js.
- AMD FidelityFX Super Resolution 1.0 (ffx-fsr/ffx_fsr1.h with ffx_a.h),
  https://github.com/GPUOpen-Effects/FidelityFX-FSR commit a21ffb8f6c13233ba336352bdff293894c706575,
  SHA-256 93c3922362ea7fc99cbcc698ca30c98de4f8c246d1fbb0b09e015ddef38ce3a5 (ffx_fsr1.h) and
  f60e2722fcd13989523b9164d776ab382b3692791767f3bf8bb19967f763f3fb (ffx_a.h). EASU and RCAS ported to WebGL2 in
  candidates.js.
- Anime4K 3.2 upscalers (glsl/Upscale/Anime4K_Upscale_CNN_x2_S.glsl and _M.glsl),
  https://github.com/bloc97/Anime4K commit 7684e9586f8dcc738af08a1cdceb024cc184f426,
  SHA-256 4c53ec2e287908f7ee7bcb266b0170421626d663576468b7d7dafc62962649a4 (S) and
  716e02098a68f0d648761f2b96b4dd139e1cb09b174bb369fca3aa34328fff7e (M). Not copied here: run_quality.mjs and
  build_device_page.mjs read the downloaded files, and the built device page carries them with their notice.
